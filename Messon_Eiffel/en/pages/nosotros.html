Who we are

Our story

Since the first coffee

A mesón with Andalusian soul

We are Mesón Cafetería de Eiffel, a corner of Motril where breakfasts smell of toasted bread and meals are cooked over real charcoal embers. It was born from a simple idea: do things right, with honest produce and no rush.

From the golden croissants of the morning to the chuletón sizzling over the night's embers, every dish carries the same care. There are no formulas here, only people.

Real produce

Acorn-fed Iberian cured meats, seasonal vegetables and our own preparations. We choose every ingredient with the same care.

Timeless recipes

Andalusian cooking without artifice. The croquettes, the toasts and the grilled meats are made just as they always have been.

A table for everyone

A place where everyone feels at home, from the first coffee of the day to the last drink of the night.
